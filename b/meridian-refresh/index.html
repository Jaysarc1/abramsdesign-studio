04The load path

One unbroken line, roof to bedrock.

Wind doesn't push a house over. It lifts it. We tie every slab to every wall to every pile, so the roof has nowhere to go.

  1. A200mph

    Every connection, slab to footing, sized for it. Code asks for 170.

  2. B9lb

    A 2×4 fired at 34 mph into every window and door. It bounces.

  3. C+3ft

    Finished floor above the base flood elevation. The county asks for one.

  4. D40ft

    Auger-cast piles, down past the sand to the limestone.

07The process

Sketch to keys, one team the whole way.

  2. 01 · Week one

    Walk the lot

    At noon. We read the sun, the wind and the water table before we draw a line.

  3. 02 · Weeks two to ten

    Draw

    Plans, sections and a sun study for every wall of glass, drawn by the people who will build it.

  4. 03 · Weeks ten to eighteen

    Permit

    We file with the county and answer every comment ourselves. You never chase a reviewer.

  5. 04 · Months five to fourteen

    Build

    Our own crews pour, frame and close it in. One superintendent, from the first pile to the last coat.

  6. 05 · Day one

    Keys

    A walk-through at noon, a binder with every spec, and ten years of us on call.

09Questions

What people ask us on the lot.

Anything not here, ask us at noon on your own site.

Book a site walk
What does a Meridian house cost?

Most of our new houses land between $650 and $950 a square foot, built. You get a fixed number before we break ground, not after.

How long from first walk to keys?

Twelve to eighteen months for a new house. Permits take three to four of those, and we carry them for you.

Will you build another architect's plans?

Rarely. The overhangs, the walls and the storm rating are drawn as one thing, so we draw them ourselves.

Do you take on renovations?

When the bones are worth keeping. If they aren't, we'll tell you honestly on the first walk.

Where do you build?

Palm Beach and Martin counties, from Tequesta down to Boca Raton, and the barrier islands in between.

What does the warranty cover?

Ten years on the structure, the roof and every opening. If water gets in where it shouldn't, we come back.

Start with the site.
