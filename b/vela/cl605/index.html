What we ask for

Before an aircraft comes off the market for you, a short letter confirming that funds are available. It is the courtesy every serious seller expects, and it is held in strict confidence.

  • A letter from your bank or custodian, on letterhead
  • Or a lender's commitment, if you are financing
  • Dated within the last thirty days
  • The amount available, never an account

When it happens

Once your letter of intent is accepted, not before. Looking, asking and flying along on a demonstration never require it.

Who sees it

The seller's counsel, and no one else. It is never forwarded, copied into a data room or kept on file after closing.

How it is used

To show the seller the offer is real. Afterwards it is returned or destroyed, whichever you prefer.

Notify me

Not this one, but something like it? Tell us what you are looking for and you will hear first when it comes to market, often before it is listed.
