Building Dreams Since 1998

A family tradition of quality, value and craftsmanship

For more than twenty years, Meridian Homes, Inc. has been proud to build beautiful new homes for families throughout Palm Beach County and the Treasure Coast. What started as a small family business building one home at a time has grown into one of the area’s most trusted names in new home construction, with over 2,400 homes built in more than 30 communities. Through all of that growth, our commitment has never changed: to build every home as if we were building it for our own family.

At Meridian Homes, we believe that buying a new home should be an exciting and enjoyable experience. That is why we take the time to listen to each of our homebuyers and guide them through every step of the process, from choosing the perfect homesite and floor plan to selecting finishes at our 6,000 square foot Design Center. Our experienced sales consultants, design coordinators and construction managers work together as one team to make sure your home is delivered on time and exactly the way you imagined it.

Every Meridian home is built with energy-efficient features, impact-resistant windows and doors, and the quality craftsmanship South Florida homeowners have come to expect from us. Our homes include many upgrades as standard features that other builders charge extra for, including quartz countertops, designer tile, stainless steel appliances, 42” cabinetry, and much more. We are also proud to offer a comprehensive 2-10 home warranty on every home we build.

Whether you are a first-time homebuyer, a growing family looking for more space, or ready to downsize into a low-maintenance villa, Meridian Homes has a community and floor plan that is right for you. Our communities offer resort-style amenities including clubhouses, fitness centers, pools, playgrounds, tennis and pickleball courts, and walking trails, all close to top-rated schools, shopping, dining and the beautiful beaches of South Florida.

We invite you to visit one of our model homes today and see the Meridian difference for yourself. Our friendly sales team is available seven days a week to answer your questions, and we offer flexible financing options through our preferred lenders. Contact us today to learn more about current incentives and quick move-in homes!
