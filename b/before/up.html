About Us

Every house has a story.

Unique Places is a boutique renovation and development company based in South Florida. We specialize in finding properties that others have passed by and transforming them into beautiful, functional homes that families will love for years to come. Our team brings together experience in construction, design and real estate, and we manage every project from start to finish, from the initial purchase and permitting through demolition, construction, finishes and final staging. We believe that a great renovation should feel like it belongs in its neighborhood, so we take the time to understand the character of each street and community before we ever put a hammer to a wall. Whether it’s a small cottage that needs a little love or a full reconstruction from the ground up, we approach every project with the same care, attention to detail and commitment to quality. We are proud to have completed projects in Boynton Beach, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Lake Worth and the surrounding areas, and we are always looking for our next opportunity to give an overlooked property a second life.
