Learn to fold

From a cup to a rose, one level at a time.

Every class starts with a single square. Five levels take you from your first fold to a rose twisted from one uncut sheet.

  1. Level 1

    The cup

    Six folds. The first thing every class makes, and it holds water.

  2. Level 2

    The fox

    A face, two ears and a grin. Your first reverse folds.

  3. Level 3

    The crane

    Eight steps that teach every fold you will need after it.

  4. Level 4

    The lily

    A petal fold, four times over, then curled open by hand.

  5. Level 5

    The rose

    A twisted spiral from one uncut square. Two hours, and worth it.
