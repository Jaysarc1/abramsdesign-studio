Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a reservation?

Not for the café. For groups of eight or more, or for the whole café after close, see catering and events.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, fast and free. The long table by the window has outlets at every seat.

Where should I park?

The Clematis Street garages are a two-minute walk, and street parking is free after 8 PM.

Are dogs welcome?

On the patio, always. Water bowls are by the door.

Do you have gluten-free or dairy-free options?

Oat and almond milk for every drink, and a gluten-free Midnight Cookie most days.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Order on Square, DoorDash or Uber Eats and pick up at the end of the bar.
