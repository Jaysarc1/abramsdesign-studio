§ 1The appeal

An appeal is not a second trial.

It is a careful reading of what already happened, by judges looking for legal error that changed the outcome.

i.

The record

Transcripts, exhibits and rulings from below. No new witnesses, no new evidence.

ii.

The brief

The case is argued in writing. Every point must be preserved and cited to the record.

iii.

The decision

A panel decides whether the error mattered, sometimes after oral argument.

A loss at trial is not always the last word.Roughly 100 appellate cases a year, in state and federal courts.
