About Chloe

My journey with fashion began long before I even realized it. Growing up, I was immersed in creativity thanks to my mom, who owned a children's clothing business called Baby Chloe's Closet. As a little girl, the hum of the sewing machine was a constant melody as she worked from early mornings into late nights, and often rocking me to sleep on her chest as she stitched. I became her tiny model, wearing her adorable creations and showcasing her love for thoughtful, handmade designs. Those early experiences planted the seeds of my appreciation for craftsmanship and style, even if I didn't know it yet.

Fast forward to 2023, I purchased my first sewing machine and dove into the art of creating clothes under my mom's guidance. What started as a personal hobby, designing outfits for myself, evolved into something much bigger: Bohemia By Chlo. Blending my love for vintage aesthetics and modern comfort, I create handmade, timeless pieces that are as unique as the people who wear them. Each design is a reflection of my passion for creativity, nurtured by years of inspiration from my mom and my love for film photography, which keeps my vision rooted in nostalgia and artistry.
